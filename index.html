The people behind the book

Written by John Cokinos and shaped by a small team of editors and collaborators.

Author

John Cokinos

John Cokinos is the founder of Viewpoint Method and the author of The Guru Theory.

For decades, John has studied one question: why two people can face the same setback and come out of it as completely different people. His answer is perspective. The way we see ourselves, our failures and the people around us shapes every decision we make, and every outcome that follows. Viewpoint Method grew out of that work: a set of practical principles for changing how you think, so that what you do, and what happens to you, can change too.

As a coach, John works with capable, accomplished people who sense that something is missing. He is less interested in quick fixes than in the patterns underneath them: the old voices we mistake for our own, the need to control, the habit of blaming circumstance.

The Guru Theory is a novel inspired by true-life elements. It follows a man who loses everything in a single afternoon and travels to the mountains of western Tibet to rebuild himself. John wrote it as a story because, in his experience, people rarely change by being told what to do. They change by recognizing themselves.

John lives in Bethesda, Maryland.

Editor

Carolyn E. Tracy

Carolyn E. Tracy has been inspiring, mentoring and coaching aspiring writers for over 20 years. She wrote the national award-winning play “In Like With Me”, the musical “A Kiss Don’t Cost a Penny”, and wrote and performed her one-woman shows “Two-Bit Ham Actor”, “On the Boards” and “Shadow of a Star.” Personal development and empathy are her lifelong passions. She resides in Portland, Oregon.

Editor

Sheldon Greaves

Sheldon Greaves is a longtime independent scholar, educator and artist whose published works cover a wide range of topics, from the ancient Near East to national security. His paintings have appeared in several Pacific Northwest art shows. He is the author of The Guerrilla Scholar’s Handbook. Sheldon resides in Albany, Oregon with his spouse and two cats.

Line editor

Steven W. Rodgers

Steven W. Rodgers is an editor with The Edit Authority. He line-edited The Guru Theory in its later drafts and contributed creative and narrative suggestions along the way. He holds an MFA from the University of Virginia and has spent some 30 years as an editor, college writing instructor, textbook author, newspaper columnist and playwright. He has written five musicals and more than 300 songs, some performed by Broadway and Netflix star Ashley Park. He is based in Virginia.

In memoriam

Russell E. Gmirkin 1954–2025

Russell E. Gmirkin was a dedicated independent scholar, researcher and author whose work brought fresh perspectives to the study of biblical history and literature. His investigations into the Hellenistic origins of the Hebrew Bible made a distinctive contribution to contemporary biblical scholarship. His background in rock climbing was vital to the development of our story.

The principles in The Guru Theory are taught in practice at Viewpoint Method, John Cokinos’s personal development program.
